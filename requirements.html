Game System Requirements

Check the hardware and software needed to play popular PC and mobile games. Minimum requirements allow a game to run, while recommended requirements generally provide a better experience.

PC Games

1. Grand Theft Auto V

Version: GTA V Legacy

OS: Windows 10 64-bit

Processor: Intel Core 2 Quad Q6600

RAM: 4 GB minimum

Graphics: NVIDIA 9800 GT 1 GB

Storage: Around 125 GB

Note: Enhanced Edition requires more powerful hardware.

2. Minecraft Java Edition

OS: Windows 10 or later

Processor: 64-bit, 4-core CPU recommended

RAM: At least 8 GB with a dedicated GPU

Graphics: Vulkan 1.3-compatible GPU with 2 GB VRAM for the published minimum target

Storage: Extra free space for the game and worlds

Note: Requirements vary with game version, shaders and mods.

3. Valorant

OS: Windows 10 64-bit or Windows 11

Processor: Intel i3-540 or AMD Athlon 200GE

RAM: 4 GB

Graphics: Intel HD 4000 or AMD Radeon R5 220

DirectX: DirectX 11 support

Note: Windows 11 also requires TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot.

Mobile Games

4. PUBG Mobile

Platform: Android and iOS

OS: Compatible Android or iOS version

RAM: 4 GB or more is a practical target

Storage: Several GB of free space

Processor: Compatible mobile chipset

Note: Actual requirements depend on the current version and downloaded resources.

5. Free Fire

Platform: Android and iOS

Android minimum: Android 4.4, dual-core 1.2 GHz, 1 GB RAM

iOS minimum: Older published guidance lists iOS 9 and iPhone 5s

Storage: At least 1.5 GB in older published guidance

Better performance: 3 GB RAM or more is preferable

Note: These are older published minimums; check current store compatibility.

6. Call of Duty Mobile

Platform: Android and iOS

OS: Compatible Android or iOS version

RAM: 4 GB or more is a practical target

Storage: Extra space for maps and downloaded content

Processor: Compatible mobile chipset

Note: Check the current app-store listing. Emulator support is not officially provided for every setup.

Action Games

7. Assassin's Creed

Example version: Assassin's Creed Mirage

OS: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Processor: Intel Core i7-4790K or Ryzen 5 1600

RAM: 8 GB

Graphics: GTX 1060 6 GB or RX 570 4 GB

Storage: 40 GB

Note: Other Assassin's Creed games have different requirements.

Sports Games

8. EA Sports FC

Example version: EA SPORTS FC 25

OS: Windows 10 64-bit

Processor: Intel Core i5-6600K or Ryzen 5 1600

RAM: 8 GB

Graphics: GTX 1050 Ti or Radeon RX 570

Storage: 100 GB

Recommended RAM: 12 GB

9. Cricket 24

OS: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Processor: Intel Core i3-2100 or AMD FX-6300

RAM: 8 GB

Graphics: GTX 770 4 GB or Radeon R9 270 4 GB

DirectX: Version 12

Storage: 60 GB

Note: An SSD is recommended, but not required.

10. NBA 2K

Example version: NBA 2K25

OS: Windows 10 64-bit

Processor: Intel Core i3-9100 or Ryzen 3 1200

RAM: 8 GB

Graphics: GTX 960 4 GB or Radeon RX 570 4 GB

DirectX: Version 12

Storage: 150 GB; SSD required

Note: Other NBA 2K versions may differ.

Before Installing a Game

  • Check the exact game version.
  • Make sure enough storage space is available.
  • Update your operating system and graphics drivers.
  • Meeting minimum requirements does not guarantee smooth gameplay.
